# The space panel

*Space → Apps → Renewal & Review Dates.*

Every watched date in the space, soonest first, with the page it came from,
the date, and how long is left.

## Mark done

A date that has been dealt with should stop being red. **Mark done**
settles it: no more reminders, and it reads as settled rather than overdue.
It does not edit the page.

**Reopen** undoes it.

A date marked done stays done even if the page is edited and the wording
around it changes.

## My reminders

Your own email subscription for this space: your address and your notice
periods. Yours alone, and it does not change what the team receives.

## Space settings

Delivery lives under *Space settings → Renewal & Review Dates*, not on this
panel, because the Slack webhook is a shared secret and Confluence
restricts that area to space administrators.
