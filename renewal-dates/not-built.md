# What is deliberately not built

These are decisions, not gaps. Each one keeps the app out of your user
directory, keeps it working without configuration, or keeps it simple
enough to run without a support desk. Asking will not change them; this
page saves you the message.

**Reminding page watchers, or any user you name.** Both need
`read:confluence-user`, the permission that hands an app every user's name
and email address. We will not hold it. The page's author and last editor
are reachable without it, and between them they cover the person who can
actually act. If your renewal owner is neither, subscribe them by email:
that address is one they gave us, not one we went and looked up.

**Reminder schemes, and an administration console.** Named, reusable
schedules exist in other apps to make up for having no detection: if a
human enters every reminder, they need somewhere to enter it. This app
reads the page, so there is nothing to configure per reminder. Notice
periods are a space setting and a personal one, and that is the whole of
it.

**Microsoft Teams.** Doing it properly means a Teams app with a bot, or a
certified Power Automate connector. Both need hosted infrastructure we do
not run. Asking you to build your own Workflows flow and paste the URL is a
half-integration that breaks the next time Microsoft changes something, and
it is you who would notice, not us.

**Editing your pages.** The app writes a comment and one hidden property.
It never changes a word of what you wrote, never inserts a macro for you,
and never moves a date.

**A dashboard, a report, an export.** The app tells the right person before
a date passes. What happened across two hundred contracts last quarter is a
different product.

**Recurring dates.** A date that repeats every year is a rule, and a rule
that lives in an app rather than in the page is a second source of truth
waiting to disagree with the first. Write next year's date on the page when
you renew.

**Anything that needs you to type a date twice.** If a feature would have
you re-enter something the page already says, it is not going to be built.
