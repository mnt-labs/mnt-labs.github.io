# On a single page

## The byline

Under the page title, beside the author, the app shows how many dates are
watched on that page and what the next one is. It is there on every page
with no insertion step.

Click it for the list: each date, what it is called, and whether it is
being watched or only suggested. A suggested date starts being watched with
one click.

It also shows what was sent and when, so "was anyone actually told" is
answerable without opening a support ticket.

## The macro

If you would rather have the list inline in the document, insert the
*Watched dates* macro. It shows the same thing as the byline panel, in the
body of the page.

The macro is optional. The byline is always there.
