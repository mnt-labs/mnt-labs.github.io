# When reminders go

## Notice periods

Space administrators choose from 90, 60, 30, 14, 7, 3 and 1 days before the
date. A reminder always goes out on the day itself, whatever else is
chosen.

Each email subscriber sets their own instead. Procurement can ask for 90
days on a contract with a three-month notice period without imposing that
on everyone.

## Keep reminding after

Off by default. Switch on one day, seven days or thirty days later, for the
case where the date passed and nobody acted.

Bounded on purpose: it stops after the offsets you chose rather than
nagging until someone dismisses it.

## Send hour and time zone

Per space, under *Space settings*. The time zone is detected from your
browser the first time you open the settings; change it if the space works
to a different clock.

A reminder that would have gone out while the app was unavailable is caught
up within a week, not dropped, and not dumped weeks late.
