## What the app does

It finds dates already written in your Confluence pages and sends a
reminder before they pass, through a comment on the page, Slack, or email.

## What it does not do

The app is a reminder. It is not a contract management system, not a
compliance record, and not legal or financial advice. It does not decide
what a date means, and it cannot know about a date that is not written in a
page it can read.

**Do not rely on it as the only control on an obligation that matters.**
A reminder can fail to arrive for reasons outside our control: a mailbox
rejects it, a Slack webhook is revoked, a page is edited so the date is no
longer there, or Atlassian's scheduled triggers do not fire. Where a missed
date has legal or financial consequences, keep your own control as well.

## Availability

The app runs on Atlassian Forge, inside Atlassian's infrastructure. Its
availability is Atlassian's, not ours, and we do not offer an SLA.

Email delivery depends on Resend and on the receiving mail provider. Slack
delivery depends on Slack and on the webhook staying valid.
